## Language: English
- Write everything (lines, title, description, hashtags, overlays) in natural spoken English.
- Calm, curious, deadpan narrator. Contractions are fine ("you're", "it's").
- Short sentences that are easy to say aloud. No tongue-twisters, no abbreviations
  the voice could misread (write "for example", not "e.g."), numbers as digits only
  when they are short ("3 seconds").
